# IntentPrompt

The entry of an agent-first workspace: a personal greeting with the state of things, a large prompt, and suggested intents.

**Markup:** `fh-workspace__greeting` (72 Black, 2rem) and `fh-workspace__sub` (one sentence, what needs attention), an `fh-prompt fh-prompt--large` with placeholder "What do you want to get done?", the generalized AI notice, and `fh-chips` of 3–5 intents with icons. Intents are tasks in sentence case ("Check delivery risks"), drawn from the person's role. Intentional addition, after SAP's intent-based Joule Work workspace.
